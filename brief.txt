ROBOT NAVIGATION BRIEF

Develop navigation logic for a mobile robot.

The robot receives information about whether the path
in front, to the left, and to the right is blocked.

It may also receive information about whether its goal
is ahead, to the left, or to the right.

The robot should prefer moving toward its goal whenever
this can be done safely.

It must never move into a blocked direction.

If there is no safe direction available, it must stop.

The navigation component will eventually be tested in
multiple simulated environments.
